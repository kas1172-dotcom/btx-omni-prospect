# Research JSON loader field-retention policy

Every field on a research JSON record must have one of three dispositions: **(a)** retained on its canonical read model (including an explicitly declared source-to-model alias), **(b)** deliberately dropped with a reason beside `DROPPED_FIELDS` in the loader, or **(c)** routed to an identified metadata/payload bag. There is no fourth category; “undetermined” is not an acceptable disposition.

`backend/tests/test_loader_field_disposition.py` enforces this for component classes, programs, BU capabilities, BTX business units, and BTX facilities. It inspects the union of fields across each collection (including the first record), compares them with dataclass fields and declared aliases, and requires every remainder in the loader's `DROPPED_FIELDS`. When a source JSON adds a field, the test fails with the collection and field name until its disposition is recorded. A declared drop is an explicit review decision, not evidence that the value is unimportant. Nested capability process descriptors are retained in `CapabilityProcessFamily` and checked by `test_read_model_field_retention.py`.

Public BTX facility identity/location fields keep their existing public provenance. The operational certification, process, and capacity overlay is authored for the demo and separately labeled `data_mode=SAMPLE`, `synthetic=true`, and `source="Authored SAMPLE operational record"`; its certification dates and capacity figures are **not verified BTX facts**. No numeric machine availability is inferred from the public company profile.
