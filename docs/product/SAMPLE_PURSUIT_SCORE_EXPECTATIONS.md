# SAMPLE pursuit score expectations

These are scenario-design expectations, not verified BTX relationships, buyer
positions, awards, capacity commitments, or company facts. The scorer's rubric
bands remain authoritative; the original per-company numeric target windows
were guidance, not contracts. No scorer weights or thresholds were changed.

The eight named pursuits use differentiated, explicitly synthetic access,
requirements, budget, price, track-record, material, margin, coordination, and
facility-load assumptions. Path A continues to derive manufacturing fit and
delivery capability, schedule, and certification from scope and facility records.

numeric window. Scores are the loaded SAMPLE state after the differentiated
