# Monitor source / event coverage matrix

Sources are selected from event coverage, authority and identifiers—not a list of arbitrary websites. Live calls are intentionally not implemented.

| Industry | Authoritative structured / official sources | Secondary / broad discovery | Detects | API / update / backfill | Identity and limitations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial Aerospace | FAA notices/data; SEC EDGAR; company IR/newsrooms; state economic-development agencies | Aviation Week and reputable trade press; broad news/RSS | approvals/regulatory changes, earnings/backlog, launches, capacity/facilities | EDGAR APIs/archives; FAA varies; newsroom RSS varies; long EDGAR backfill | CIK, ticker, official domain; releases may omit legal entity or program |
| Defense | SAM.gov, USAspending, DoD service/contract announcements, Federal Register/GovInfo | Defense One, Breaking Defense, trade press | solicitations, awards, modifications, supplier/program signals | SAM/USAspending APIs; daily/near-real-time; historical backfill available | UEI, CAGE, award/notice IDs; classified work and subawards often absent |
| Space | NASA procurement/news/awards, SAM.gov, Federal Register, Commerce/NOAA where relevant | SpaceNews, trade press, RSS | awards, solicitations, program launches, grants, partnerships | agency APIs vary; SAM backfill available | award/notice IDs, CAGE/UEI, mission/program IDs; commercial launches may be incompletely disclosed |
| Semiconductor | Commerce CHIPS announcements, SEC EDGAR, state/local economic-development agencies, official IR | Semiconductor trade press, broad discovery | capex, fab/capacity/new facility, grants, supply-chain/M&A | EDGAR API/archive; agencies vary; historical releases often available | CIK, legal entity, facility geography; project timing and downstream suppliers can be vague |
| Medical Device | FDA/openFDA and FDA databases, SEC EDGAR, official FDA notices/company newsrooms | MedTech trade press | approvals, recalls/regulatory change, product launch, M&A, facility signals | openFDA APIs and historical datasets; EDGAR archives | FDA product/510(k)/PMA identifiers, CIK; regulatory clearance does not prove commercial launch |
| Robotics | SEC EDGAR, company IR/newsrooms, state/local economic-development agencies | Robotics Business Review, trade press, broad news/RSS | launches, partnerships, funding/M&A, facilities, earnings | EDGAR API/archive; newsroom feeds vary | CIK, official domain, facility address; private-company funding and entity identity may be incomplete |

For every adapter: require a stable source-system ID, source record ID or canonical URL, official publisher/domain rules, source-native ID mapping where available, declared polling cadence, retention/backfill bounds, and a documented failure/coverage limitation. Authoritative structured records outrank official publisher records; secondary and broad discovery create leads requiring evidence and corroboration.
