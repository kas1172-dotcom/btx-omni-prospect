# Public signal confidence

`modules/scoring/public_inputs.py` adapts canonical Monitor events and their bound observations into the existing Signal Confidence family. `monitor/briefs.py` supplies the same assessment to the shared seller card and includes it in synthesis invalidation. It does not change publication permissions, commercial scores or risk severity.

`BTX_PUBLIC_SIGNAL_INPUTS_POC_1` is provisional configuration: registered source tiers 1/2/3/4 map to 100/90/70/40; resolved canonical identity maps to 100; specificity measures typed event/title/actual event date/resolved subject; publication freshness uses the existing source-specific hours. An unknown/future publication date is missing. Independent corroboration remains unknown until explicit independent source lineage exists; repeated articles do not raise it. Source tier does not prove every assertion is true. The canonical family continues to enforce coverage and evidence requirements. These bins are POC assumptions, not empirical reliability measurements or Jamie approval.

The adapter does not yet establish scoped risk impacts, commercial applicability or independent corroboration. Those must come from their canonical evidence owners before scored outputs can be published. A high-confidence public announcement does not establish a BTX order, capacity or affected supplied component.
